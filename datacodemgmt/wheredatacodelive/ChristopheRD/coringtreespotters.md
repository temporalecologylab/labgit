## CoringTreespotters
This is the WDC for the project involving the phenology data collected by the treespotters along with the cores that will be collected in April 2025. It will be included in Christophe's Chapter 2

**Important:** main and original repo is [here](https://github.com/temporalecologylab/treespottersOG.git)
#### Last Modified:  
29 October 2026
#### Date of Study
2015 to December 2025. 
##### Date of Public Archiving:
Spring 2027 (estimated)
##### Goal
Understand how growth and growing season length relate for mature trees.
